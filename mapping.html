How a row is filled
  • Untick Show to leave a row out of the datasheet table and of the Product Options tree.
  • Leave Response empty to print the datasheet value as it is.
  • Type a sentence to print the same standard response every time.
  • Remove text takes text out of the datasheet value: + PE turns 380V/3Ph/60Hz + PE into 380V/3Ph/60Hz. Separate several texts with ;.
  • Put $ or * where the datasheet value should go: Panel thickness is $ Thermal break prints Panel thickness is 62 mm Thermal break.
  • Spec keywords are used by the record page to find this row's clause in a specification. Separate entries with ;. An entry finds a clause when every word of it starts a word of the clause: fin spac finds "fins shall be spaced". Empty means the row is not searched for. How the keywords are weighed and which rows a clause may take: Matching rules.
  • Name in Excel changes what is printed in the Component column. Empty keeps the datasheet name.
  • In Product Options: Auto decides by the row's name. Option = what the unit is made of or comes with (waits for approval, shown per model). Duty figure = a figure of one selection, such as a flow or a pressure (shown as lowest to highest, never waits). In General / per model says where an option shows; the Product Options page moves rows too (Edit, Move to general / Move to columns).
