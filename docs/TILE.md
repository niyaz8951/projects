# Projects tile

Tile id `projects` - repo `projects` - address `/projects/` - schema `projects` - functions `public.projects_*` - private bucket `projects` (not created yet, see Data).

## What it does
The team's Projects tool, moved from Thinkneering feature for feature. A project holds the datasheets and specifications of one job: the datasheet PDF is read in the browser into a table of values per unit; the specification PDF is turned into clauses; under each clause the record shows what helps answer it (the project's datasheet values, else the factory's approved Product Options, and the library's approved answer); people answer the clauses, answers reach the factory's compliance library through review, and the compliance Excel files are downloaded. No AI anywhere.

Today (steps 1-3) the register, the project page, the datasheet reader and the specification tool are built: a datasheet PDF (AHU, FCU) is read in the browser into a table of values per unit and saved in the project; a specification PDF or text is turned into clauses, saved in the project and in the factory library, and read and filled on the record page with the three helps under every clause (the library's answer pre-filled in green, the datasheet value as a blue chip, else what to select from the approved Product Options as a dashed chip); Focus mode fills one clause at a time; the two compliance Excel files and the project Excel download. Compliance review (step 4) is built: the reviewers answer the factory library, take or turn down the users' answers, upload and download the library; an approved answer pre-fills the record in green. Product Options (step 5) has its button in place, saying "Coming in step 5" until it lands. A user sees and works in **his own projects and the projects shared with him**; a **tile admin and the super user** see and work in every project and share projects with other users of the tile. Everything a page shows is data from Supabase.

## Rights
- `edit` - create projects and change their details; read datasheets, convert specifications, answer clauses, download the Excel files (steps 2-3) - in projects the user may open.
- `review` - the knowledge base: Compliance review (approve answers into the library, Internal Comments, upload / download the library) and Product Options (approve values, groups, extras, hidden values, the row mapping) (steps 4-5). Given to nobody unless the tile admin decides; tile admins and the super user hold it by the core rule.

A user with the tile and no right reads his projects only. The owner enters the same list in Admin > Tiles.

## Who sees what
- A plain user: the projects he created and the projects shared with him (list, open, edit with `edit`); he cannot share a project on. Another user's project answers `NOT_FOUND`, never `NO_RIGHT`, so nothing tells him whether an id exists.
- A tile admin (Admin > Tiles > access, "admin") and the super user: every project of every user; the list shows a "Created by" column and the intro line says so. Only they share a project (the whole list of people, as in the old tool); the people offered are the active users of the tile, other than the creator.
- The database decides (`projects.can_open`); the page only hides columns and buttons.
- Later steps inherit this: a datasheet table, a specification record or a download is reached only through a project the caller may open. Compliance review and Product Options are reached with the right `review` (tile admins and the super user).

## Pages

Datasheet notes" and "2. Compliance records" with their empty states ("No datasheet yet" + *Read a datasheet*, "No specification yet" + *Add a specification*); details with "Edit details" (right `edit`); "Shared with" with the Share sheet (tile admins and the super user); history. On a phone the Next step's first button is also docked at the bottom. The datasheet buttons and tile lead to `datasheet.html`; "1. Datasheet notes" lists the project's tables (View, Download Excel, Remove with `edit`, "Read another datasheet"); the tile turns blue once a table is saved and the Next step moves to "Step 2 of 2 - Add the specification". The specification buttons open "Coming in step 3" until step 3 lands; a reader without `edit` sees the texts and no buttons.
- `datasheet.html?project=<uuid>` - **Read a datasheet** (right `edit`, a project the user may open): choose the product (AHU and FCU have readers; Chiller says it is not ready), choose or drop one or more PDFs; the PDF is read on the device with pdf.js (`vendor/pdfjs/`), `ds-parse.js` / `ds-fcu.js` rebuild the table (one column per unit, sections, sub-headings, differences from the first unit highlighted), the row mapping decides which rows show and what they are called; the table is saved in the project at once ("in place of the earlier table of the same units" when it covers them), Download Excel (`ds-xlsx.js`), Cancel while reading, "Only rows that differ", the reader's warnings, files that could not be read, and the site warnings (selection location and power-supply frequency against the project's region). Rows new to the mapping are added to it with the starter keywords; the values of the mapped rows go to Product Options of the factory the datasheet names, "to approve".
- `note.html?id=<uuid>` - one saved table (anyone who may open its project): facts, find, "Only rows that differ", Download Excel. The old row-name button that shows what the factory offers comes with Product Options (step 5).
- `add-spec.html?project=<uuid>` - **Add a specification** (right `edit`): product and factory (the project's newest datasheet pre-selects both; a warning when another factory is chosen), the PDF (read on the device, up to 50 pages) or pasted text, "Skip the cover and contents pages", *Convert and start filling*: `cm-engine.js` makes the rows (Part, section, lettered and numbered items, Sr), the product check asks when the text clearly names another product (an FCU specification saved as AHU), `projects_run_save` saves the record and opens it.
- `record.html?run=<uuid>` - **the compliance record** (anyone who may open the project reads; `edit` fills): the specification as a document (Read) or with boxes (Edit); show (all, to fill, filled, waiting for review), find, the count line, the legend; under each clause the three helps; Enter goes to the next clause; one answer for a whole section; Split and Join up; Internal Comments (right `review`) and TL Review (tile admins); **Focus mode** (one clause, the six answers, Remarks, Next to fill; Clause and Page views; on a phone the record opens in it); Download Excel (the consultant's four columns, `_DRAFT` in the name while a clause is open) and Download working Excel (colours, sources, sheet 2 = Datasheet rows, or the Selection list when rows have no datasheet value). The precedent line names the earlier record of the same factory that shares the most clauses.
- `compare.html?project=<uuid>&product=<id>` - **Specification beside datasheet**: what the specification asks, next to what each unit has, row by row; find, only the rows asked about, Excel.
- `project.html` also: "2. Compliance records" (per product: open, progress, badges, Upload filled Excel, Remove), "Specification beside datasheet" links, **Download project (Excel)** (information, Specs-/Notes-/Review- sheets per product), the Next step through the records (Fill / Continue, Read the datasheet, Up to date). The register's "Saved" column counts the records, tables and answers in review.

## Browser storage and markup
- `localStorage` / `sessionStorage`: none. The pages keep nothing in the browser (the session and the theme are the core's, under `dame.`); the old reader's "last product" in `sessionStorage` was dropped, the project's last datasheet pre-selects the product instead.
- `localStorage` (view settings only, under `dame.`): `dame.projects.focus-view` (Focus mode's Clause / Page view) and `dame.projects.focus-scale` (its text size).
- Markup is built with `Core.el` and `textContent` only on the tile's own pages; the pages brought over from the old tool (add a specification, the record, compare, Focus mode) build theirs with `Hub.el` from `assets/pr-hub.js`, which is `Core.el`'s rule in the old call shape: `textContent` only, no `innerHTML` (the shim throws on it), a `style` string turned into `node.style` properties (the CSP forbids `style=""` attributes). No inline scripts. The files brought over as they were (`ds-parse.js`, `ds-fcu.js`, `ds-read.js`, `ds-xlsx.js`, `pr-xlsx.js`, `cm-engine.js`, `cm-rows.js`, `cm-highlight.js`, `cm-focus.js`, `xlsx-writer.js`, `pr-match.js`, `reflow.js`, `xlsx-lite.js`, `cm-product-check.js`) touch no markup beyond that rule; what changed in them: `ds-read.js` and `pr-add-spec.js` (the worker path), `cm-highlight.js` (the rules file path), `cm-focus.js` (its styles moved to `pr-legacy.css`, its storage keys), `pr-match.js` (returns the rules). Colours in the Excel files are Excel's, not the page's.
- `assets/pr-legacy.css` - the old site's component stylesheet, scoped to `body.pr-legacy` (only the brought-over pages carry that class) with every token pointed at the core theme and the tile accent, as the Tools tile's `tl-legacy.css`; the pages' own styles and the Focus pane's are in it too. The tile writes no colour of its own.
- `data/highlight-rules.json` - the keyword dictionary the record's highlighting uses (configuration, from the old tool).
- `vendor/pdfjs/` - pdf.js (Apache 2.0, licence beside it), the copy Thinkneering used; the worker is `/projects/vendor/pdfjs/pdf.worker.min.js`, no network calls.

## Data
- `projects.projects` - `id`, `code` (`P-<year>-<number>`, unique, never reused, never changes), `name`, `client_type`, `client_name`, `region`, `created_by` + `created_by_name`, `created_at`, `updated_by` + `updated_by_name`, `updated_at`. RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.members` - who a project is shared with: `project_id`, `user_id`, `added_by` + `added_by_name`, `added_at`. The creator is never a member. RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.events` - what happened in a project (`created`, `details changed` with the changed fields), shown as History. RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.lists` - the pick lists `client_type` and `region` (generic values: kinds of client, countries). RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.counters` - one counter per name, only goes up (`project` for the codes). RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.notes` - one table read from a datasheet: `project_id`, `user_id` + `username`, `product_id`, `factory_id` (the factory the datasheet names, `''` when unknown or several), `file_names`, `units`, `row_count`, `columns` (jsonb, the unit tags), `rows` (jsonb, `[{ section, component, cells, marks, kind, key }]`), `bytes`, `created_at`. The PDF is never stored. RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.dn_map` - the row mapping, one list per product: `row_key` (`section|sub|component`), `section`, `sub`, `component`, `show`, `label`, `strip` (Remove text), `response`, `keywords`, `sort_order`, `reviewed` (false = "New" for the editors), `in_tree`, `kind` (`''` / `option` / `duty`), `place` (`''` / `general` / `model`), `updated_by`, `updated_at`. `projects.dn_map_settings` - per product, `show_unmapped`. `projects.dn_starter_keywords` - the starter keywords and Remove text per product and row (68 AHU rows, 38 FCU rows, from the old tool after its migrations 0005 and 0013; configuration only). RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.po_values` - Product Options: per `factory_id`, `row_key`, `model` (FCU unit model, `''` for AHU), `grp` (the group: the rows of one filter, one fan), `value_key` (lower case), `value`, `times`, `hidden`, `first_at`, `last_at`, `approved` (**false** when collected from a datasheet; a value already approved for the row keeps its approval in a new group), `approved_by`, `approved_at`. At most 300 values per row, factory and model. RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.runs` - one converted specification: `user_id` + `username`, `product_id`, `factory_id`, `source` (`pdf` / `text`), `file_name`, `line_count`, `unique_count`, `matched_count`, `project_id` (null once the record left its project), `created_at`. `projects.run_lines` - every row of it: `seq`, `type` (part / section / letter / number / text), `sr`, `spec_text`, `line_id`, the project's own `compliance` / `remarks` / `answered_by` / `answered_at`, `review` (`''` / pending / approved / rejected), `tl_review`. `projects.lines` - the factory compliance library: one line per clause and factory (`norm_hash` = md5 of the normalised text), `spec_text`, the library's `compliance` / `remarks` / `comments` (Internal Comments; text after `#` is for reviewers only), `status` (open / answered), `times_seen`, where it was first seen (Part, section, `home_run` / `home_seq`, Sr). `projects.answer_log` - the library answer's history. The PDF is never stored. RLS on, no policies.
- `projects.products` - `id` (`ahu`, `fcu`, `chiller`), `name`, `sort_order`, `active`; `projects.factories` - `id` (`ahu-dubai`, `ahu-riyadh`, `fcu-shenzhen`, `fcu-riyadh`, `chiller-italy`, `chiller-jeddah`), `product_id`, `name`, `sort_order`, `active`. Seeded from the old tool's `cm_products` / `cm_factories` (configuration, no internal data). The datasheet tables, specifications, library and Product Options of steps 2-5 hang on them. RLS on, no policies.
- Bucket `projects`: **not created in step 1** - nothing uploads yet. It comes with the first step that stores files, with policies tied to the project id (`<project-id>/...`) and `core.has_tile`.

## Functions
- `projects_lists()` - the pick lists. Anyone with the tile.
- `projects_products()` - the active products, each with its active factories, in order. Anyone with the tile.
- `projects_dn_rules(p_product_id)` - the row mapping of one product (`show_unmapped`, `rules`). Anyone with the tile (the reader needs it). `INVALID:product_id`.
- `projects_dn_add_rows(p_product_id, p_rows)` - right `edit`: adds the rows the mapping has not seen (first of a repeated key wins, shown according to the product's setting, `reviewed = false`, starter keywords and Remove text), never touches rows already there; at most 3000 rows a call (`INVALID:rows`). Returns `{ added }`.
- `projects_po_collect(p_product_id, p_items)` - right `edit`: the values of a datasheet into Product Options, `{ factory (name), key, value, model, grp }` per unit and row, at most 20000 a call (`INVALID:items`). Ignores an unknown factory, a row the mapping does not show or leaves out of the tree, and the project rows (`general||project`, reference, material name, selection software, report date, product, factory, unit); a set of filter sizes "2x(592x592x48) 1x(287x592x48)" is saved as its sizes; a value seen again counts once more; a new value is "to approve". Returns `{ saved }`.
- `projects_note_save(p_project_id, p_product_id, p_file_names, p_columns, p_rows, p_factory)` - right `edit`, a project the caller may open (`NOT_FOUND` otherwise): saves the table; an earlier table of the same product whose every unit is in this one is replaced; `p_factory` is the name the datasheet carries, matched among the product's factories. `INVALID:columns`, `INVALID:rows` (also when the rows are over 12 MB), `INVALID:product_id`. History line "datasheet notes saved / replaced", one audit line. Returns `{ id, replaced, factory_id }`.
- `projects_note_get(p_id)` - the table with its rows, project and product, if the caller may open the project; `NOT_FOUND` otherwise.
- `projects_note_delete(p_id)` - right `edit`, a project the caller may open: deletes the table; history line "datasheet notes deleted".
- `projects_run_save(p_project_id, p_factory_id, p_source, p_file_name, p_lines)` - right `edit`, a project the caller may open: saves the converted rows (at most 8000), puts each body clause of 8+ characters into the factory library once (first sighting keeps Part, section and place), returns `{ run_id, lines, unique_lines, matched, answers }` with the answers and Internal Comments the library already holds. `INVALID:factory_id`, `INVALID:lines`. History "specification converted", one audit line.
- `projects_run_get(p_run_id)` - the record: every row with the project's own answer and the library's, `can_comment` (right `review`), `can_review` (tile admin), `can_edit`, the precedent. `NOT_FOUND` for a record outside a project the caller may open.
- `projects_run_save_line(p_run_id, p_seq, p_compliance, p_remarks, p_check)` - right `edit`: the answer typed for one row (equal to the library's = no review, else `pending`; both empty = the project's own answer taken away). `p_check` = the first 40 letters of the clause; `INVALID:check` when the rows changed since the page was opened. One history line "compliance filled online" per person, record and hour.
- `projects_run_fill_section(p_run_id, p_seq, p_compliance, p_replace, p_check)` - right `edit`: one answer for every clause of a section; `''` takes the project's answers off it.
- `projects_run_save_review(p_run_id, p_seq, p_text, p_check)` - tile admin or super user: the TL Review of one clause (stays in the project). `projects_run_save_comment(p_run_id, p_seq, p_comments, p_check)` - right `review`: the Internal Comments of the clause in the library.
- `projects_run_split(p_run_id, p_seq, p_rows, p_check)` / `projects_run_join(p_run_id, p_seq, p_check)` - right `edit`: the rows change, never the words (`INVALID:rows`, `INVALID:join`); an answer on a changed row goes back to review; a library line made only by a wrong row is dropped.
- `projects_run_delete(p_run_id)` - right `edit`: the record leaves the project (it stays in the library's history); pending answers are withdrawn.
- `projects_run_submit_answers(p_run_id, p_rows)` - right `edit`: the answers read from a filled Excel land on the matching rows by text.
- `projects_po_approved(p_factory_id)` - anyone with the tile: the approved, non-hidden Product Options values of a factory with the row's kind (what the dashed chips and the Selection list are built from in the browser, `pr-picks.js`).
- `projects_export(p_project_id)` - anyone who may open the project: everything for the project Excel; history "project downloaded".
- `projects_lib_lines(p_factory_id, p_status, p_search, p_part, p_topic, p_client_type, p_client_name, p_project_code, p_limit, p_offset)` - right `review`: one page (at most 300) of a factory's library in reading order (Part 1-3, section groups by their lowest number, one block per conversion, the order in that specification; with a project code its own order), with the counts, the Part / topic groups, the client and project-code lists, and the users' answers per clause (the six newest, waiting first). `INVALID:factory_id`.
- `projects_lib_save_answer(p_line_id, p_compliance, p_remarks, p_comments)` - right `review`: the library's answer at once (`answer_source = 'admin'`, logged); both empty puts the line back to open; `p_comments` null leaves Internal Comments.
- `projects_lib_delete_line(p_line_id)` - right `review`: a junk line leaves the library (rows that held it can no longer be answered); audited.
- `projects_review_decide(p_run_id, p_seq, p_decision, p_line_id, p_compliance, p_remarks)` - right `review`: `approve` makes the user's answer (or the corrected one) the library's and settles the same answer waiting on other records of the clause; a line that already has another answer is kept (`INVALID:answered`); `reject` leaves it in its project. `INVALID:check` when the row no longer holds the line the reviewer saw, `NOT_FOUND` when nothing waits there. History line in the project, one audit line.
- `projects_lib_import(p_factory_id, p_file_name, p_rows)` - right `review`: a filled compliance sheet into the library (at most 20000 rows; answers in their standard words - "By contractor" is In Contractor's Scope -; last row of a clause wins; a new clause lands in its Part and section; an upload replaces an existing answer and says so); one `library-upload` run; returns the counts.
- `projects_lib_runs(p_limit, p_offset)` - right `review`: conversions and uploads, newest first, with the project code. `projects_lib_export(p_factory_id, p_project_code)` - right `review`: the library for the Excel.
- `projects_lib_reset(p_product_id, p_factory_id, p_with_projects)` - the super user only (`NO_RIGHT` otherwise): empties a factory's library and its history; clauses used by records stay empty and the users' answers on them go back to review; with `p_with_projects` the product's records in projects go too. Audited.
- `projects_list` also returns `notes`, `runs` and `pending` (counts per project); `projects_get` returns `notes` (the tables, newest first, without their rows) and `runs` (the records with their counts: clauses, filled, answered, pending, approved, rejected).
- `projects_list(p_q, p_region, p_client_type)` - the register with `members` (count), `total` and `sees_all`; own and shared projects, or all for an admin; `p_q` matches name, client name or code. Anyone with the tile.
- `projects_create(p_name, p_client_type, p_client_name, p_region)` - right `edit`; returns `{ id, code }`. `INVALID:name`, `INVALID:client_type`, `INVALID:client_name`, `INVALID:region`.
- `projects_get(p_id)` - one project with its members (names through `core.display_names`), `can_share` and its last 100 events, if the caller may open it; `NOT_FOUND` otherwise.
- `projects_update(p_id, p_name, p_client_type, p_client_name, p_region)` - right `edit`, own project or admin (`NOT_FOUND` otherwise); writes one history line listing the changed fields; no change, no line.
- `projects_share_get(p_id)` - tile admin or super user (`NO_RIGHT` otherwise; `NOT_FOUND` when the caller cannot open it): the members and the people the project can be shared with (`core.tile_users('projects')`; until the core has it, `users` is empty and `core_missing` is true).
- `projects_share_set(p_id, p_user_ids)` - tile admin or super user: the whole list; every id must be an active user with the tile (`INVALID:user_ids`), at most 200, the creator is skipped; adds and removals are history lines (`shared with @a, @b` / `sharing ended for @c`) and one audit line.
- `projects.can_open(profile, project)` - internal helper: creator, member, tile admin or super user.

## Decisions
- 2026-11-04 - **Features learned from the old tool** (`Thinkneering`, `tools/projects`; no code, data or layout copied): a project is name + client type + client name + region, with a code `P-<year>-<number>` that is never reused; the register has find and filters (region, client type), a count, a download of the list, and each project a details form and a history. The old tool's "share with a person" is replaced by DAME access: everyone with the tile sees every project, which is what a team register is for. Its "created by" filter is not built; the column is shown instead.
- 2026-11-04 - Pick lists are data (`projects.lists`), so the owner can add a region or client type with one SQL line rather than a page change.
- 2026-11-04 - Usernames are stored next to the ids (`created_by_name`, `updated_by_name`, `events.username`) so lists need no join into `core`; `core.display_names` can refresh them later if renames ever matter.
- 2026-11-04 - No delete in step 1. A project will hold maps, reviews and options; what deletion does to those is decided when they exist.
- 2026-11-04 - Tile colour: blue until the owner chooses one in Admin > Tiles.
- 2026-11-05 - **Own projects; admins see all** (owner). Replaces "everyone with the tile sees every project" from step 1: a user sees only the projects he created; tile admins and the super user see every project. `db/projects/002_visibility.sql`. The old tool's "share a project with a colleague" is not built; an admin can work in any project, which covers the hand-over case. Sharing can be added later as a `projects.members` table if the team asks.
- 2026-11-05 - *(superseded the same day by "Move the tool as it is", below; kept as the record of what was learned)* **A project comes with its tools; "Maps" are rule and knowledge maps, not locations** (owner). The flow learned from the old tool:
  1. **Datasheet reader**: a product datasheet PDF (AHU, FCU; chiller later) read in the browser into one table, Section / Component / one column per unit, differences between units marked; the rows shown and their wording come from the **row mapping** (one list per product: show, name, spec keywords, remove text, standard response; it grows by itself with rows no admin has seen). The table is saved in the project; the values of the shown rows are collected per **factory** (read from the datasheet) into Product Options.
  2. **Product Options**: per product and factory, the tree Section > Component > values seen on datasheets, with editors' special options and notes, values taken out, rows left out; FCU trees per model as tables; Excel. Also the home of the row mapping.
  3. **Compliance review**: a specification (PDF or pasted text) converted into clauses, Part / section / clause; a **library** per product and factory of every clause met with its answer (normalised text is the key; Internal Comments with `#` rules); a project **record** to read and fill (six answers, remarks, full-screen one clause at a time on a phone), Excel down and up; users' answers reach the library only through an editor's "Use this answer". For each clause the page shows **suggestions**: the library answer on an exact match, the datasheet row its spec keywords point at (a number in the clause compared by code with the datasheet value, units converted; a class on its scale), earlier answers in the same record, and the rules from the maps.
  4. **Maps**: trees that grow left to right (map and outline views, notes, special notes, images, links, import/export as a plain outline, verified / to verify). Two kinds: **knowledge** (product and process understanding) and the **rule book**, whose branches are rules read by code like if / else if: `When: <words>` - `Section: <words>` - `Part: 1|2|3` - `Unless: <words>` - `Answer: <one of six>` - `Remark: <text>`, in a fixed decision order (datasheet -> library -> earlier answer -> rule -> other projects). No AI model in DAME: the rule book is the whole engine.
  5. The project page ties it together: a "Next step" card, tool tiles (blue when the project holds that data), the records and tables of the project, "Specification beside datasheet", history, and "Download project" as one Excel.
- 2026-11-05 - *(superseded, see below: rights are `edit` and `review`; maps are out)* **One tile for the whole flow.** Datasheet tables, product options, the compliance library and the maps all live in schema `projects` and bucket `projects`: the contract forbids a tile to read another tile's tables, and compliance suggestions read all three. Each part gets its own right (declared when its step is built: `library` for the compliance library and reviewing answers, `options` for product options and the row mapping, `maps` for managing shared maps; `edit` stays for projects and records). The PDF reader (pdf.js) is copied into `vendor/` with its licence; PDFs are read in the browser and the datasheet PDF itself is never uploaded; a specification PDF is stored in the bucket only if the owner wants it kept.
